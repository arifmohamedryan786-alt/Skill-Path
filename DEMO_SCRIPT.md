# SkillPath – 5-Minute Live Demo Pitch Script

**Presenter:** Lead Product Architect / AI Engineer  
**Target Audience:** Judges, University Deans, Recruiters, and Student Mentors  
**Total Duration:** 5 Minutes  
**Primary Scenario:** Arif Mohamed, 3rd Year B.Tech AI & Data Science student: *"I don't know what to do after college."*

---

## ⏱️ Minute 0:00 – 1:00 | The Problem & The Core Mission

### Spoken Script:
> *"Good morning, everyone. In universities today, over 70% of engineering and science students reach their third year with one overwhelming anxiety: **'I don't know what to do after college.'**
>
> Traditional learning platforms ask them: 'Which career path do you want? Choose your framework!' But an anxious 20-year-old student doesn't know what a tech stack is, doesn't know industry jargon, and gives up before they start.
>
> That is why we built **SkillPath – Your Career Path, From Zero to Job-Ready**.
>
> SkillPath is designed with a strict beginner-first philosophy:
> 1. It assumes **zero prior knowledge**.
> 2. Terms are written at a **Grade-8 reading level** with plain-language hover definitions.
> 3. And if a student has no skills or projects yet, **'None yet' is always a valid answer.**
>
> Let's experience this through **Arif Mohamed**, a 3rd-year AI & Data Science student who feels stuck and undirected."*

### Screen Actions:
1. Open `http://localhost:5173` on the login screen.
2. Highlight the warm linen background (`#F7F6F2`), the editorial serif typography (`Fraunces`), and the 1-click demo switcher.
3. Click on **"Arif Mohamed"** to log in instantly.

>

> *'You enjoy finding patterns in numbers and you are studying AI & Data Science, so AI/ML Engineer and Data Analyst both fit your natural strengths.'*
>
> Arif clicks on **'Day in the Life'** to see what an AI Engineer actually does in simple words, tests out the 1-hour **'Try it Task'**, and selects **AI/ML Engineer** as his target goal."*

## ⏱️ Minute 2:00 – 3:00 | The 7-Stage Roadmap & "How to Learn This"

### Spoken Script:
> *"Now that Arif has a goal, what happens next?
> On other platforms, students are abandoned with a playlist of 40-hour videos.
>
> On SkillPath, our **Roadmap Planner Agent** immediately generates a complete **7-Stage Zero-to-Job Roadmap**:
> From Stage 1: Explore & Foundations, to Core Skills, Projects, Internships, Interview Prep, and Final Placement.
>
> Because Arif is in Year 3, the timeline automatically adjusts to focus on internships and interview-ready portfolios.
>
> And for **EVERY SINGLE SKILL**, SkillPath provides a **'How to Learn This'** card:
> - What it is without jargon.
> - A learning method: *'Spend 30% watching videos, 70% writing code.'*
> - The exact beginner resources curated in order: interactive tutorial, video, and mini exercise.
> - And crucially: **'Common mistakes to avoid'** and **'How to know you've learned it'**.
>
> Arif doesn't need to wonder what to do today. The **'Your First Week'** sprint lays out day-by-day 30-to-60 minute micro-tasks."*

## ⏱️ Minute 3:00 – 4:00 | Checkpoints, Cryptographic Passport & Flight Simulator

### Spoken Script:
> *"After studying, Arif takes a **Checkpoint Test**.
>
> Our **Assessment Agent** evaluates both multiple-choice concepts and practical coding tasks:
> - If Arif scores below 50%, the platform doesn't give up on him. It dynamically injects **remedial refresher tasks** directly into his roadmap!
> - But when Arif scores 70% or higher, something special happens:
>
> SkillPath issues a **Verified Skill Badge** to Arif's **Skill Passport**.
> Look at this public page: `/passport/arif-mohamed-0001`.
>
> Each verified skill includes a **cryptographic SHA-256 tamper-evident fingerprint** generated from Arif's ID, the skill, score, and timestamp.
> Anyone—a recruiter, college mentor, or company—can click **'Verify Hash'**, and our backend recomputes the cryptographic hash live to prove the badge hasn't been faked!
>
> Next, Arif wants to know: *'When will I actually be ready for a job?'*
> He opens the **Career Flight Simulator**.
> Using our predictive velocity formula:
> $$\text{Hours} = (\text{Target} - \text{Current}) \times \text{BaseHours} \times \text{Difficulty} \times \text{LearnerPace}$$
>
> Arif drags the slider from 10 hours to 20 hours a week:
> Instantly, the line chart recalculates, pulling his job readiness date forward by two whole months! Arif clicks **'Apply this plan'**, and his sprint updates immediately."*

### Screen Actions:
1. Navigate to `/assessments` and show the checkpoint quiz.
2. Navigate to `/passport`.
3. Click **"Verify Cryptographic Fingerprint"** and show the instant green verification badge.
4. Click **"Copy Public Link"** or **"Download Certificate PDF"**.
5. Navigate to `/simulator`.
6. Drag the weekly hours slider from 10 to 20 hrs/week and watch the projection curve and date update live.
